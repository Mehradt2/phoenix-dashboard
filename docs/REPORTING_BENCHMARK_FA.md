# Reporting Benchmark — KulePoshti

Benchmarked concepts used in the product:
- Executive KPI overview followed by drill-down.
- Trend over time rather than static score only.
- Risk distribution.
- Failure Pareto / root-cause analysis.
- Individual performance profiles.
- Prioritized coaching actions.
- Human-review visibility.
- No synthetic seed data.

## Information hierarchy
1. What changed?
2. Is it risky?
3. What behavior/rule drives it?
4. Who/which interactions are affected?
5. What action/coaching is required?
6. Did performance improve after action?

## Views
### Executive
Volume, quality average, open review, critical rate, quality trend, risk mix.

### QA Lead
Rule Pareto, critical findings, calibration drift, open review aging.

### Team Lead
People table, recurring weaknesses, coaching priorities, review history.

### Individual
Calls, trend, recurring rules, evidence examples, coaching history.

The pattern intentionally follows enterprise QM systems that combine automated coverage, scorecards, manual review, coaching and drill-down instead of presenting a single opaque score.
